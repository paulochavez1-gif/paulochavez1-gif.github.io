How I work

Three ideas I keep coming back to

Go where users already are

Recivo has no app because restaurants already live in WhatsApp. Less friction beats more features.

AI reads, code decides

I use models for what they do well and deterministic rules for what cannot fail: money, taxes and dates.

Actually finished

A product isn't done when it works on my laptop. It's done with a domain, backups, alerts, legal terms and someone using it.

About

Between product, marketing and AI

I'm Mexican, with a degree in Business Strategy and Transformation from Tecnológico de Monterrey. I've worked in e-commerce operations at Rappi, in ISO quality management and in investment analysis, in Mexico and the United States.

I now live in Barcelona, where I'm studying the MSc in Digital Marketing and Generative AI at INESDI. Recivo starts from a simple question: would a small business use AI if it didn't have to learn anything new?

I took it from a bot on my laptop to a live product with an official WhatsApp number, its own server and legal terms for two countries. I built it with AI-assisted development: I decide what gets built, how it's designed and when it's good enough, and I use coding agents to move faster without lowering the bar.

I'm looking for teams shipping AI products into the real world, and for businesses that want to try Recivo.
