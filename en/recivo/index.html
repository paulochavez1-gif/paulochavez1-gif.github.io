In 43 seconds

From a receipt photo to a quarter ready for the accountant

A photo on WhatsApp, AI reading, checks in code, a dashboard that updates itself and the final export. The video is in Spanish, with on-screen text and no sound.

The problem

Receipts get lost in wallets, and the quarter comes anyway.

RestaurantsPaper supplier invoices, daily cash-register closes, and an accountant who wants everything at once every three months.
FreelancersDeductible expenses that never get recorded because opening an accounting app is a chore.
StudentsNo idea where the month's money went, and no intention of filling in a spreadsheet.
What they shareWhatsApp is open on their phone all day. Recivo lives there.
Architecture

Product and engineering decisions

The hard part wasn't getting the AI to read. It was not trusting it.

Zero silent errors

A misread value nobody notices is worse than an error. Every field goes through deterministic rules; anything that doesn't add up is re-read up to twice and, if it still fails, the user is told on WhatsApp. An unreadable total is never saved.

The same receipt once came back as 2026 and once as 2024. Since then the date is copied exactly as printed, parsed separately, and both readings must agree.

The AI talks, the code calculates

The assistant answers on WhatsApp through a fixed set of tools. Numbers come from a pure, tested module, never from the model. Edits use the same validations as the dashboard, and deleting requires pressing a button.

"How much VAT have I paid this quarter?" → VAT lookup tool → exact sum from the database.

Never invent a tax

A screenshot of a bank notification came back with 10% VAT that appeared nowhere. Now, with no printed breakdown, the expense is stored as all base and no deductible VAT. Conservative, and it never under-declares.

A Mexican supermarket receipt with IEPS excise tax looked inconsistent. "Other amounts" joined the equation so a correct value would never be "fixed".

Built for bursts

A restaurant sends ten photos in a row at closing time. A per-phone queue keeps the order, a global limit on AI calls switches model when one is saturated, and there's a single acknowledgement per burst.

Automated test: 8 photos at once → 8 saved, 1 acknowledgement, 8 confirmations in order.

Getting to production

A product you can sell, not just a demo

Security and accounts

  • Multi-tenant, isolated by session, never by an id sent from the client
  • scrypt passwords, persistent rate limits and single-use reset tokens
  • Strict CSP, Origin-based CSRF protection and security headers

Operations

  • Own server with HTTPS and domain
  • Locked deploys with backup, health check and automatic rollback
  • Daily backups and email alerts if WhatsApp, the database, the AI or the disk fail

Legal

  • GDPR privacy policy and full Mexican privacy notice (LFPDPPP)
  • Versioned terms accepted at every signup
  • Data processing agreement and complete account deletion

Technology

Few pieces, well chosen

Node.js with no frontend framework and SQLite on a single server: cheap to run and easy to reason about while the product finds its fit. Built with AI-assisted development: I defined the product, the architecture, the business rules and the quality bar, and directed every iteration.
